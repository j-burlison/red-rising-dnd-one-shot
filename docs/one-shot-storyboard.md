# "Benediction of Dis" — One-Shot Storyboard

**Premise:** Obsidian and Gray operators under a Peerless Scarred Gold are sent to Dis, capital of Dispater, ostensibly to extract intel on troop movements — actually to retrieve **the Chimera Codex**, a spellbook enabling cross-color breeding (colors bargain with devils to circumvent the Society's genetic caste laws).

## Key NPCs
- **The Gold (Peerless Scarred)** — Paladin; mission commander, true orders, demands the book be secured for the Society
- **Gray Troupe Leader** — Ranger; secretly a Rising agent, has been recruiting/scouting since Act 1, turns openly in Act 3

---

## Act 1 — The Benediction (aboard The Concordia, orbit above Farun)
- **The Grays' overlook room:** the session opens with the Gray squad in a room overlooking the ceremony hall. **Ryn briefs the Grays** there with the cover story (troop-movement intel), banter follows, and the Gray players introduce their characters in character
- **Cut to the floor:** the Obsidians kneel on one knee beside a standing Servian while the Whites perform the Benediction, with unease in the air (Dis has been "too quiet"). The Obsidian players introduce their characters as narration or inner voice. The Obsidians get no brief, only orders when it's time. Servian gives no brief at all.
- Roleplay caste dynamics: Obsidians can't speak out of turn, Grays needle at the edges
- **The turned backs:** when the Whites name "Maartog, son of Slate," the kneeling Obsidian NPCs turn their backs on him in a ritual shunning. He kneels, but never bows his head to a Gold.
- **Servian's brothers:** as the ceremony ends, Karnas au Ferrox (massive, a brute) and Cagney au Ferrox (skinny, lithe, a trickster) press Servian: it has to be him, because no one will suspect him, and the Sovereign and the family's honor are depending on him. *(Optional: Maartog, kneeling nearby, overhears. A hint, not the objective.)*
- **Show the oath working:** a small order an Obsidian visibly can't disobey, or a PC flinching when tempted to
- Short skill scene (Insight/Persuasion/Deception) — establish who's suspicious of the mission
- **Break:** hellish leechcraft breach through portals in space, devils board **The Concordia**
- **"All units to launch bays. Prepare for planar jump and Iron Rain."**
- Both squads reach the corridor between cargo hold and launch bay together — ship shakes, hull visibly getting cut into, gravity fails, roll initiative
- **Servian and Ryn fight alongside the party** in the corridor. Servian's gravity boots let him ignore zero-G until they fail.
- **Round 1:** leechcraft carves through the hull; Barbtail Skirmishers and the first Ash-Bound Wretches pour in. **Round 2:** the Iron Ward Enforcers follow with the rest of the swarm.
- **Mid-fight escalation:** leechcraft tears free (Enforcer's death, a countermeasure, a player reaching its control panel, or at the start of round 4) — one sharp round of vacuum pull + debris, not ongoing
- **Zero-G Corridor:** the escape route is a physical puzzle layered on the fight (see Lore & Mechanics for full rules) — anchors, movement-by-push, and attack recoil throughout
- **End condition:** enemies dead, shuttle bay door opens
- **The planar jump that follows is a short rest** — HD, short-rest features, Stem Injectors, and Pulse Fists all refill before the landing hazard on arrival. This is also what makes the Iron Rain hot and early.
- **Hazard:** NPC pods shot down around the PCs; failed save = fall damage on arrival at Dis
- **Warlock beat:** as the pods fall into Dis, every warlock's patron screams in their head, shocked to find them suddenly in the realm of Hell (Irina and any other warlock)

**Knowledge split (set up before the session):** Irina is a Rising agent like Ryn and knows the true objective (her orders put the Logbook first, the Codex second); Smack has a hint (Ryn asked him to have her back, and anything she says about breaking something is his cue). Servian tells no one. Maartog and everyone else believe it's a standard intel-extraction mission.

## Act 2 — Iron Rain onto Dis
- Landing scattered across a district of Dis: black iron architecture, screaming stonework, bruised orange sky, devils being actively broken by Society occupation
- Stated objective: retrieve intel on troop movements from the Dis Arcane Library
- **The approach is open:** sneak in, talk in, or go in by force (force just starts the guard fight early). The devils know a Society attack is coming, but not that this group is after the Codex and the Logbook. Smack's infiltration skills are one quiet option; nothing depends on them.
- **Discovery beat — The Logbook:** in the archivist's chambers/records vault adjacent to the objective, PCs find a bound ledger (Infernal) recording every use of the Chimera Codex: Color, date, requesting House, and the soul-cost paid to devil brokers. Entries show low Colors trading their own souls, and both Colors trading the souls of their slaves. One entry names a Gold House that shares the strike team's Gold's family name — implied to be their cousin. A **loose older page** (about 20 years old) tucked in the back records House Ferrox selling Slate's tribe, 40 Obsidian souls, and branding the chief an oathbreaker: proof Maartog's father was framed. Servian finds it with his cousin's entries and learns his House has been doing this for a generation.
  - Two paths to the discovery: Investigation/stealth (find and translate the ledger directly) or Deception/Insight against a cagey archivist/guard NPC who denies knowledge
  - **The archivist is Irina's devil broker:** he can see the soul fragment she already sold, calls her "Administrator" in front of the squad unless she shuts him down, and privately tells her the term is due, offering to forgive the remainder if the Codex stays in Dis
- **Irina carries the Logbook** out of the vault (she may be the one who finds it; otherwise Ryn or Servian orders her to carry it)
- **Combat encounter:** library guards — lighter resistance than expected, which reads as its own unease/warning sign before Act 3
- **Ryn takes the Codex** once the pedestal's ward is down; she needs it in hand for Act 3

## Act 3 — The Twist and Extraction
- **The Logbook demand:** on the way to the shuttle, at the Extraction Plaza, Servian stops and demands the Logbook so he can burn it. Irina is carrying it, and refusing is defiance that breaks her first sigil. Servian can offer Maartog the Slate page, and Slate's name restored before the Sovereign, if he stands with him.
- **"Break the chains":** Ryn answers with the Rising call sign. Irina recognizes it at once. Smack recognizes his cue, then realizes his closest friend has lied to him for ten years and is calling in his promise.
- **The Gray troupe leader turns**, reveals the full truth to everyone, sides against the Society, and **throws Smack the Codex**: "Get this to the Rising, whatever happens to me."
- **The horde pours in:** Dis's devils, drawn by the alarm and the attack, flood the plaza at Ryn's reveal, hostile to the Society and never to Ryn
- **The Gold orders the party to kill the Gray and the devils** — every PC (Obsidian and Gray alike) is Oathbound and must actively break the oath to defy this order
- **Climax combat:** PCs choose a side — fight for the Gold, fight for the Gray/Rising, or break the oath entirely and run with the book.
- One-shot ends at the close of this combat

### Three Endings
1. **Society wins** — Gold retains the book, Gray/Rising forces defeated
2. **Rising wins** — Gray/Rising forces secure the book, Gold defeated/driven off
3. **PCs escape with the book** — party breaks the oath, abandons both sides, flees Dis with the Chimera Codex in hand
