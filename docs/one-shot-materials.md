# "Benediction of Dis" — One-Shot Materials

## Naming Convention
Golds carry House names ("au [House]" — e.g., *Servian au Ferrox*). All other Colors take their Color as a surname (e.g., *Ryn Gray*).

## NPCs

### The Gold — Servian au Ferrox, Peerless Scarred
**Class:** Paladin | **Combat role:** heavy melee, razor-wielder in Act 3, Bred to Command support ability

A Peerless Scarred commander, bred and trained since birth for exactly this kind of operation — decisive, controlled, and utterly convinced of the righteousness of the Society's order. Doesn't relish cruelty, which makes him scarier: every order he gives in Act 3, including "kill the Gray," is delivered with total calm, because to him it isn't murder, it's maintenance of the system that keeps the galaxy — and now the planes — from chaos. (Play him with a Cassius au Bellona-style cold composure: precise, unshaken, dangerous specifically because he never seems to struggle.)

- **Personal stake:** knows the true objective from the start — the mission is to retrieve the Chimera Codex and bring the cross-color breeding pipeline fully "in house." He believes the book was meant to be *destroyed*, not exploited; he does not know the true scale of its use.
- **The cousin twist:** when the Logbook surfaces his cousin's House, Servian is genuinely **surprised and disgusted** — this is a real crack in his composure, one of the only moments in the one-shot where his control visibly slips. Play this as a rare, brief flash of real emotion before he re-forms his mask and doubles down on the mission (or, if you want more ambiguity, lets it visibly shake his certainty going into Act 3).
- **Roleplay hook:** clipped, formal command language even under pressure; never raises his voice
- **In Act 3:** draws a razor once the party's loyalty is in question — the "boss reveal" of the fight

### Gray Troupe Leader — Ryn Gray, Rising Agent
**Class:** Ranger | **Combat role:** skirmisher, razor-wielder in Act 3, leads the Rising/devil defense

Been running this troupe long enough that the Grays trust her instincts without question — which is exactly what let her quietly recruit and vet the party over the course of the mission without anyone noticing. Genuinely cares about the Grays under her command; the betrayal in Act 3 isn't cold calculation like the Gold's orders, it's personal. She's seen enough of what deals like the Logbook cost the people at the bottom of the hierarchy, low Colors and slaves alike, and she's decided she's done being complicit. (Play her with a Holiday-style bluntness and loyalty — plainspoken, protective of her people over any chain of command, quietly formidable rather than showy about it.)

- **Personal stake:** knows the true objective via Rising channels from the start; has likely been probing 1-2 PCs' loyalties since Act 1's ceremony scene
- **Roleplay hook:** talks to Grays and Obsidians alike as people rather than ranks — the contrast between her command style and Servian's is worth playing up throughout
- **In Act 3:** reveals the truth when the devil horde arrives, draws a razor of her own, makes it clear she's not asking the party to follow her — just choose

### Devil Patrol Leader (Act 2, Library Guards)
**Suggested type:** Bearded devil or similar mid-tier fiend, reskinned/renamed as Dis city watch
Not expecting a fight — this is a guard captain used to ally-status routine, not an invasion footing, which is exactly why the Act 2 encounter should read as "lighter than expected." Could have a brief, almost confused moment before combat (Insight-worthy) — they genuinely don't understand why Society forces are attacking.

### Key Petitioners / Slaves (Logbook context, Act 2 flavor)
Non-combat NPCs encountered near the library or archive — souls or servants (mortal petitioners bound to Dis, or Society slaves brought along for labor) who can be a source of the moral wrinkle. Consider one who's overheard something about the Logbook and can point PCs toward it, or who simply reacts with fear/resignation when PCs appear — a small human (or petitioner) cost to the whole system, made visible.

## NPC Stat Blocks

### Servian au Ferrox
*Medium humanoid (Gold), lawful neutral — CR 6 (2,300 XP)*

- **AC** 20 (Pulse Armor + Pulse Shield) | **HP** 130 (14d10+42) | **Speed** 30 ft.
- **STR** 20 (+5) **DEX** 12 (+1) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 13 (+1) **WIS** 14 (+2) **CHA** 18 (+4)
- **Saving Throws** Wis +5, Cha +7, Con +6 (Peerless Instinct)
- **Skills** Athletics +8, Insight +5, Persuasion +7
- **Damage Resistances** slashing damage from razors (Pulse Field)
- **Senses** passive Perception 12
- **Languages** Common, Infernal

**Bred to Command (3/Short Rest).** Servian grants an ally within 30 ft. advantage on its next attack roll, saving throw, or ability check.

**Aura of Protection.** Servian and allies within 10 ft. add +4 (his Cha modifier) to saving throws.

**Lay on Hands.** Servian has a pool of 35 hit points; as an action, he can restore hit points to a creature he touches, up to the pool's remaining total.

**Peerless Reflexes (1/Day).** If Servian fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Pulse Shield.** A kinetic field projector worn on Servian's off-hand arm, contributing +2 AC (included above).

**Multiattack.** Servian makes two razor attacks.

**Razor.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d8+5 slashing damage. A razor's cut ignores the AC bonus granted by the target's worn armor (shields and magic bonuses still apply).

**Divine Smite.** When Servian hits with a razor, he can expend a spell slot to deal an extra 2d8 radiant damage (+1d8 per spell slot level above 1st).

**Pulse Fist (3 charges/Short Rest, Bonus Action).** Servian slams the Pulse Shield into the ground or air, unleashing a 15-ft. cone of concussive force from his position. Each creature in the cone makes a Dexterity save (DC 15); on a failure, a creature takes 2d6 force damage and is pushed 10 ft. away from Servian; on a success, half damage and no push.

**Gravity Boots.** Magnetized boots standard-issue to Gold officers for shipboard and void operations, functioning mechanically like *Boots of Flying* — while active, Servian has a flying speed equal to his normal speed and ignores the Zero-G Corridor's push-off/anchor rules entirely, moving freely along any surface. Limited to 2 charges (as the source item), recharging after a long rest — not unlimited.

*DM Note: consider having Servian's gravity boots run out of charges or take damage during the Act 1 corridor fight (a debris strike, a solid hit, or the breach event) — disabling them mid-encounter. This puts even Servian on equal footing with the push/anchor rules for the rest of that fight, and the damaged boots can carry forward as a small flavor detail into Act 3 (a visible dent, a slight limp, a sparking heel) without needing further mechanical effect there, since Act 3 has normal gravity.*

### Ryn Gray
*Medium humanoid (Gray), chaotic good — CR 6 (2,300 XP)*

- **AC** 16 (Duro-Steel Medium armor + Dex) | **HP** 85 (14d8+14) | **Speed** 35 ft.
- **STR** 12 (+1) **DEX** 18 (+4) **CON** 14 (+2) **INT** 12 (+1) **WIS** 16 (+3) **CHA** 12 (+1)
- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +7
- **Skills** Perception +9, Stealth +7, Survival +9
- **Senses** passive Perception 19
- **Languages** Common

**Pack Tactics.** Ryn has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of her allies is within 5 ft. of that creature and isn't incapacitated.

**Tracker's Eye.** Ryn has advantage on Survival checks to track creatures.

**Fleet-Footed.** Ryn's initiative bonus is increased by 2 (already reflected in stat block conventions if rolling initiative live).

**Hunter's Mark (1/Short Rest, no slot required).** As a bonus action, Ryn marks a creature she can see within 90 ft. Her weapon attacks against the marked creature deal an extra 1d6 damage.

**Multiattack.** Ryn makes two attacks, using her Pulse Blade or Razor (see below), her Pulse Rifle, or a combination.

**Pulse Blade (longsword).** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d8+4 slashing damage (versatile, 1d10+4 if used two-handed). Ryn's visible, "legal" weapon for most of the encounter — swap to her Razor for the Act 3 reveal.

**Razor (Act 3 Reveal).** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d8+4 slashing damage. A razor's cut ignores the AC bonus granted by the target's worn armor (shields and magic bonuses still apply). *DM note: hold this weapon back until Ryn's betrayal is revealed — she fights with the Pulse Blade up to that point, and drawing the Razor should land as a visible escalation at the table.*

**Pulse Rifle.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d8+4 piercing damage.

**Pulse Grenade (3, thrown, Action).** Ryn hurls a Pulse Grenade at a point within 60 ft. Each creature within 15 ft. of that point makes a Dexterity save (DC 15); on a failure, a creature takes 3d8 force damage; on a success, half damage.

---
*(Both statted for a level-5 party facing level-7-equivalent NPCs; adjust HP down slightly if the party is expected to fight both simultaneously without splitting off against devils/each other.)*

**DM Note — Weakening Servian before Act 3:** If you want the party to have a realistic shot at siding with Ryn and winning, have Servian personally engage the Act 2 library guards — spend a Lay on Hands charge healing an ally, use Pulse Fist once or twice, or even take a smite on a guard. He should walk into Act 3 visibly capable but not at full resources, which sells the fight as winnable without changing his stat block on the fly.

## Monster Stat Blocks
Original homebrew devils, styled for Dis's iron-and-ash aesthetic. Three tiers below scale from disposable fodder to squad leader.

### Ash-Bound Wretch (fodder)
*Small fiend, CR 1/8 (25 XP)*
- **AC** 11 | **HP** 11 (2d8+2) | **Speed** 20 ft.
- **STR** 10 (+0) **DEX** 11 (+0) **CON** 12 (+1) **INT** 3 (-4) **WIS** 8 (-1) **CHA** 3 (-4)
- **Damage Resistances** cold | **Damage Immunities** fire, poison
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Languages** understands Infernal, can't speak

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d4 slashing damage.

*Ash-Bound Wretches swarm in numbers — deploy in packs of 3-6 as filler threats around a stronger devil.*

### Barbtail Skirmisher (mid-tier, ranged/flying harasser)
*Small fiend, CR 2 (450 XP)*
- **AC** 14 (natural armor) | **HP** 33 (6d6+12) | **Speed** 20 ft., fly 40 ft.
- **STR** 14 (+2) **DEX** 15 (+2) **CON** 14 (+2) **INT** 6 (-2) **WIS** 12 (+1) **CHA** 8 (-1)
- **Damage Resistances** cold | **Damage Immunities** poison
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages** understands Infernal, can't speak

**Multiattack.** The Skirmisher makes two claw attacks, or one claw attack and one tail spike attack.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d4+2 slashing damage.

**Tail Spike.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 20/80 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d4+2 piercing damage.

### Iron Ward Enforcer (squad leader)
*Medium fiend, CR 3 (700 XP)*
- **AC** 15 (natural armor) | **HP** 55 (10d8+10) | **Speed** 30 ft.
- **STR** 16 (+3) **DEX** 14 (+2) **CON** 15 (+2) **INT** 9 (-1) **WIS** 11 (+0) **CHA** 12 (+1)
- **Damage Resistances** cold | **Damage Immunities** poison
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages** Infernal

**Multiattack.** The Enforcer makes two glaive attacks.

**Glaive.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d10+3 slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution save or take 1d6 poison damage from the glaive's barbed edge.

**Rally Cry (Recharge 5-6).** The Enforcer barks a command; up to three Ash-Bound Wretches or Barbtail Skirmishers within 30 ft. that can hear it gain advantage on their next attack roll before the end of their next turn.

### Act 1 — Ship-Boarding Party (Leechcraft breach)
Suggested composition for a level-5 party (hard encounter, sets an early-session tone):
- 4x Ash-Bound Wretch
- 2x Barbtail Skirmisher
- 1x Iron Ward Enforcer (squad leader)

*Deploy in waves rather than all at once — the corridor from cargo hold to launch bay is a good excuse to stagger devils in small groups as the party moves, keeping the "fighting through" feel rather than one static room fight.*

### Act 2 — Library Guards (lighter than expected)
Deliberately undertuned — sells the "ally, not warzone" reframe:
- 2x Barbtail Skirmisher
- 2x Ash-Bound Wretch

*No Enforcer present — these are Dis's peacetime library security, not soldiers. Consider having them hesitate or ask questions before combat breaks out, reinforcing that they didn't expect an attack.*

### Act 3 — Final Horde
Scale this up or down live depending on how the party splits between Servian and Ryn:
- 3x Ash-Bound Wretch
- 3x Barbtail Skirmisher
- 2x Iron Ward Enforcer

*These represent Dis's own devils reacting to the betrayal in real time — chaotic, alarmed, and hostile to the Society specifically. If the party sides with Ryn, consider having 1-2 Barbtail Skirmishers fight alongside the party against Servian rather than against everyone equally.*
