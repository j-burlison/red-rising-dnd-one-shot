The Society — Deployment Orders

YOU HAVE BEEN CALLED

The legions of the Society are massing for another conquest. An Iron Rain has been called against the Nine Hells, and your Dominus has answered the call.

Your orders: board the interplanar dreadnaught The Concordia, and fall from the heavens upon Hell.

This page is everything you need to build your operative before we sit down at the table.

The World, In Brief

The Society rose by conquering plane after plane, binding worlds and peoples beneath a single Color-based hierarchy. Where you rank in that hierarchy — your Color — is stamped into you at birth: your training, your gear, your legal rights, even your access to magic.

You are not Gold. You are the ones Gold sends. Your team is being deployed aboard the interplanar dreadnought The Concordia, currently in orbit above the planet Farun, staged for a mission into Dispater — the plane of iron and law, and the city of Dis at its heart. Beyond that, you know only what your orders tell you.

Every operative carries something the Society implanted at birth: two sigils, one on each hand, binding you to obey the direct spoken word of your commanding Gold. You've never had reason to test them. Yet.

Build Your Operative

  1. Choose your Color
    You'll play either a Gray or an Obsidian — the two Colors on this strike team. Review both Gray and Obsidian caste cards before deciding; each comes with its own ability score bonuses and unique traits.

    GRAY

    Fast, tactical, pack-hunters. Soldiers, scouts, and infiltrators. +2 Dex, +1 Con.

    OBSIDIAN

    Huge, unbreakable, bred for the front line. +2 Str, +1 Con.

  2. Choose your class
    Your Color opens up a set of class options that fit its training:
    Gray: Rogue, Ranger, Fighter, or Warlock (a quiet pact made off the books)
    Obsidian: Barbarian, Fighter, or Sorcerer (a rare, unspoken bloodline trait)
    Any race is fine except Fiends — Color is a social rank, not a species. Level 5 to start.
    This game runs on D&D 5th Edition (2014 rules).
  3. Gear up
    Check the Gear Codex for what's standard-issue to you: Duro-Steel Armor and Stem Injectors are yours by default. Pulse Armor, Gravity Boots, and Razors belong to Gold — you won't start with them, but you'll see them.
  4. Know your Oath

    Every operative — Gray or Obsidian — is sigil-bound to obey the direct orders of their commanding Gold. Defying an order costs you: real pain, and real consequences. This isn't a suggestion in the fiction — it's baked into your character from the moment you take the field. Come to the table ready to feel that weight.

  5. Name yourself
    Only Golds carry House names. Everyone else takes their Color as a surname — a Gray named Ryn is simply Ryn Gray. Pick a first name and build from there.
  6. Bring a hook
    You don't need a novel's worth of backstory — just answer one question: why did you take this deployment? Loyalty, ambition, survival, or something you haven't said out loud. That's enough to start.

Curious About Other Colors?

Gray and Obsidian are your options for this one-shot, but the Society is built on fourteen Colors, not two. Below are dossiers on a few others you might encounter or hear referenced — including Gold, the caste that commands you, and Red, the lowest tier and the foundation everything else stands on. These are non-playable for tonight's mission, and only a fraction of the full hierarchy. In a full campaign, you'd be able to play any Color you want.
