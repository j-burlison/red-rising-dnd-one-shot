GRAY
"Quiet feet. Quick eyes. First to see, first to strike, last to fall."

Grays are the Society's soldiers, scouts, and enforcers — raised in packs, trained to track, hunt, and move as one. Where Obsidians break the line, Grays make sure the line never needed to hold in the first place.

Traits
Ability Score Increase — +2 Dexterity, +1 Constitution
Pack Tactics — advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one other Gray/ally is within 5 ft. of it
Tracker's Eye — advantage on Survival checks to track; proficiency in Perception if not already proficient
Fleet-Footed — +2 bonus to Initiative
