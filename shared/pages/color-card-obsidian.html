OBSIDIAN
"Unbreaking. Unbending. The wall that does not fall."

Bred huge, strong, and hard as the stone they're named for, Obsidians are raised in the harshest environments the Society could find, forged to endure what would kill anyone else. Bonded from birth to a Gold house, they are the tip of every spear the Society throws.

Traits
Ability Score Increase — +2 Strength, +1 Constitution
Unbreaking — advantage on saves vs. exhaustion; resistance to damage from extreme environments (cold, heat, falling)
Bred for the Line — +1 to melee damage rolls, or advantage on Athletics checks to shove/grapple/climb
War Chant — bonus action, Concentration; grants fear immunity to self and nearby allies, and can frighten weak foes each turn
Bonded Frame — counts as one size category larger for carrying capacity/grapple purposes
Playable Caste Tier · War-Bound
