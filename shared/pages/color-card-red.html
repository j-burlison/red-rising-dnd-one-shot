The Society — Caste Dossier
RED
"They dig so the sky can shine. They break so the towers won't."

The lowest Color, Reds make up the vast bulk of the Society's labor force — the hands that pull resources out of the ground and haul them to the surface. Bred for endurance over excellence, kept ignorant of the worlds their labor sustains, and paid in just enough to keep working. The Society doesn't rule Reds through fear alone — it rules them through belief, feeding each generation a lie about the world they're building toward.

Low Reds
Work the mines — underground, generations deep, extracting ore and rare elements in brutal, lightless conditions. Rarely see the surface. The hardest lives in the Society, and the least likely to ever leave them.
High Reds
Surface labor — construction, agriculture, hauling, and maintenance. Physically demanding but not the crushing subterranean grind of the mines. A marginally better lot, though still firmly at the bottom of the hierarchy.
