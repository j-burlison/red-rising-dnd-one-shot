GOLD
"Bred to rule. Bred to command. Bred to bear the weight none other could carry."

The highest Color, Golds are engineered for excellence in body and mind alike — tall, strong, sharp, and utterly convinced of their right to lead. They fill the ranks of commanders, Peerless Scarred officers, and the great Houses that steer the Society's course across every conquered plane.

Traits
Ability Score Increase — +1 to two scores of choice, +1 Intelligence
Bred to Command — once per short rest, grant an ally within 30 ft. advantage on their next attack roll, save, or check
Peerless Instinct — proficiency in one saving throw of choice
