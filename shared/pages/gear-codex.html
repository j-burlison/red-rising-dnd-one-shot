GEAR CODEX

The Society — Standard & Restricted Equipment
DURO-STEEL ARMOR
UniversalArmor

Standard-issue armor across every Color below Gold. Rugged, mass-produced, and reliable — the backbone of every Gray and Obsidian loadout.

Maps directly to standard armor types at equivalent AC (Light / Medium / Heavy).
No special property — fully vulnerable to a razor's armor-ignoring cut.
STEM INJECTORS
UniversalArmor-Integrated

Built into every suit of armor in the field, Duro-Steel and Pulse Armor alike. No medic, no potions to track — just a needle and a prayer.

3 charges per short rest.
Bonus action to trigger.
Each charge functions as a Greater Healing Potion (4d4+4 healing).
PULSE BLADE
UniversalMelee Weapon

A kinetic-edge longsword, standard issue for close-quarters work. Doesn't ignore armor like a razor does — but it doesn't require Gold training to swing, either.

Martial melee weapon, versatile. 1d8 slashing (1d10 two-handed).
PULSE RIFLE
UniversalRanged Weapon

A long-barreled kinetic rifle favored by scouts and skirmishers for precision work at range — steady hands and a clear line are all it asks for. Standard issue where a blade won't reach.

Martial ranged weapon, ammunition, heavy, two-handed. Range 150/600 ft. 1d8 piercing.
PULSE FIST
UniversalKinetic Device

A shield- or gauntlet-mounted concussive emitter. A solid slam clears space fast — useful for breaking up a crowd or buying a round to breathe.

3 charges per short rest. Bonus action to use.
15-ft. cone of concussive force. Each creature in the cone makes a Dexterity save (DC 15): on a failure, 2d6 force damage and pushed 10 ft. away; on a success, half damage and no push.
PULSE GRENADE
UniversalThrown Device

Standard-issue kinetic ordnance. Small enough to carry a few, loud enough that you'll wish you'd worn ear protection.

Thrown, range 60 ft. Action to use.
Each creature within 15 ft. of the target point makes a Dexterity save (DC 15): on a failure, 3d8 force damage; on a success, half damage.
Maximum 2 per player — this is a starting-loadout cap, not a per-rest charge.
RAZOR
Gold OnlyMelee Weapon

Neurally-linked, impossibly sharp, and terrifying to face — a razor doesn't just cut through armor, it cuts through the idea that armor matters. Never seen outside trained Gold hands.

Martial melee weapon, finesse, light. 1d8 slashing.
Ignores armor: treats the target's AC as if unarmored (Dex + shield/magic bonus only).
Requires attunement or a minimum Int/Dex to wield without penalty.
Natural 20: DM may rule a severed limb or disabled body part.

Whip Mode (item interaction to toggle, free): reach 10 ft., 1d6 slashing, loses light property. Still ignores armor. Instead of damage, a hit can attempt to disarm or trip at range using the attack roll.
PULSE ARMOR
Gold OnlyHeavy Armor

The only real answer to a razor. Golds don't win fights by being harder to hit — they win by being harder to cut.

Heavy armor, equivalent to Plate (base AC 18).
Pulse Field: resistance to slashing damage from razors specifically.
GRAVITY BOOTS
Gold OnlyFootwear

Magnetized boots issued to Gold officers for shipboard and void operations. In zero-G, everyone else is fighting the room — a Gold in these just walks through it.

Functions as Boots of Flying: while active, flying speed equal to normal speed; ignores zero-G push-off/anchor rules entirely.
2 charges, recharging after a long rest.
AEGIS SHIELD
Gold OnlyOff-Hand / Kinetic Device

A kinetic field projector worn on the off-hand arm. Deflects blows before they land — and doubles as the housing for a Pulse Fist emitter.

+2 AC while worn.
