THE SOCIETY
"Order above all. Hierarchy is not cruelty — it is the shape that keeps the void from swallowing us whole."

The Society rose by conquering plane after plane, binding worlds and peoples beneath a single Color-based hierarchy. Where other realms warred against it, Dis allied — two hierarchies recognizing kinship in each other. That alliance has lasted generations. Today, that changes.
