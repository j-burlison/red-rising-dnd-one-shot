BLUE
"They see the paths between worlds that no one else can chart."

Blues are the navigators and pilots of the Society's fleets, bred with an uncanny spatial and mathematical sense — able to plot courses through the void, and now, through the planar rifts between realms. Rarely seen outside a ship's helm, a Blue on the ground is a Blue far from home.
