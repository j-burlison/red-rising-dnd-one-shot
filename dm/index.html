BENEDICTION OF DIS

Run the session top to bottom from this page. Every scene has its checks, encounter numbers, devil stat blocks, and time budget inline. Servian's and Ryn's stat blocks open in a new tab.

Overview

Premise · who knows what · session clock

Premise. Obsidian and Gray operators under Servian au Ferrox, a Peerless Scarred Gold, drop onto Dis, capital of Dispater. Their stated orders are to extract intel on troop movements. The real target is the Chimera Codex, a spellbook that lets Colors bargain with devils for cross-color breeding, getting around the Society's caste laws. Dis has long been a Society ally, so the Iron Rain is a betrayal, not a follow-up to a war.

Who knows. Servian knows the true objective from the start. Ryn Gray, the Gray Troupe Leader, knows it through Rising channels and is a Rising agent. Only 1–2 PCs (Servian's confidants) learn it in Act 1. Everyone else believes it's intel extraction until the Logbook in Act 2.

DC 13 DEX check or save from the source notes  ·  DC 13 INT (Investigation) proposed DC, not in the original notes. Adjust freely.

Act 1 — The Benediction

Aboard The Concordia, in orbit above Farun · ~55–65 min
1.1The Benediction Ceremony
20 min · done by ~0:30
Show players: The Concordia ↗
  • Cold open. A formal ceremony. The Obsidians kneel for Servian's blessing while the Grays check gear at the margins. There's unease in the air because Dis has been "too quiet."
  • Caste roleplay. Obsidians can't speak out of turn. Grays needle at the edges.
  • Servian. Cold, Cassius-style composure. Clipped, formal command language, and he never raises his voice. He knows the true objective.
  • Ryn. Holiday-style bluntness. She talks to Grays and Obsidians as people, not ranks, and the contrast with Servian is worth playing up. She's quietly probing 1–2 PCs' loyalties during this scene.
  • The confidants. Pull 1–2 PCs aside: the real objective is the Chimera Codex. Everyone else hears "troop-movement intel."
  • Show the oath working. Give a small order that an Obsidian visibly can't disobey, or let a PC flinch when tempted to. Players should know the sigils are watching before Act 3. Full rules are in Scene 3.1.

Skill scene: who's suspicious of the mission?

  • DC 12 WIS (Insight) The briefing is incomplete. Servian is holding something back.
  • DC 15 WIS (Insight) Ryn is sizing up specific operatives, not just checking gear. This seeds Act 3.
  • DC 14 CHA (Persuasion or Deception) against Servian. He won't name the objective, but he confirms there's more to this mission than intel and that it's above their clearance.
  • Ryn talks to anyone who speaks to her plainly, with no check needed. She asks more questions than she answers.

Break. Hellish leechcraft breach through portals in space and devils board The Concordia. Shipwide: "All units to launch bays. Prepare for planar jump and Iron Rain."

1.2Breach in the Zero-G Corridor
35–45 min · done by ~1:15
Battle map: Zero-G Corridor ↗ Show players: devil art ↗

Both squads reach the corridor between the cargo hold and the launch bay together. The ship shakes, everyone watches the hull get cut open from outside, and gravity fails. Roll initiative.

Encounter — Ship-Boarding Party

The corridor

25 ft. wide, 120 ft. long, 15 ft. ceiling, with a gentle bend. Handholds (anchors) every 10 ft. on both walls and the ceiling. The breach opens on the south wall in round 1. The cargo hold is at one end and the launch bay at the other. The map has no hidden info, so it's safe to screen-share.

Allies — fighting alongside the party

Servian au FerroxAC 20 · HP 130stat block ↗ His gravity boots (2 charges) let him ignore zero-G. Have them run out or get damaged mid-fight by a debris strike, a solid hit, or the breach event, so he's grounded like everyone else. The damaged boots can carry into Act 3 as flavor. Ryn GrayAC 16 · HP 85stat block ↗ Fights with her Pulse Blade and Pulse Rifle. Her Razor stays hidden until Act 3.

Rule of thumb: Skirmishers = PCs + 2. Enforcers = 3 (2 at three PCs). Wretches = 6.

Zero-G rules (whole fight)
  • Movement. No walking. Push off a surface with a DC 12 STR (Athletics) check to drift your speed in a straight line until you hit something or reach an anchor. Changing direction needs a new anchor.
  • Anchors. The handholds every 10 ft. define the viable route.
  • Recoil. Any weapon attack, melee or ranged, made while not braced against an anchor pushes the attacker 5 ft. directly away from the target after it resolves. Bracing cancels it.
  • Adrift. A creature left unbraced after a failed push-off, a recoil, or the vacuum pull is Adrift until the start of its next turn. Melee attacks against an Adrift creature have advantage.
  • Devils move and reposition freely and are never Adrift.
  • Caste flavor. Obsidians push off harder and resist the pull (Strength). Grays thread debris and stick anchor grabs (Dexterity).

Round by round

  1. Round 1. The leechcraft carves fully through the hull. The Skirmishers and the first Wretches pour through the breach.
  2. Round 2. The Enforcers follow with the rest of the Wretches.
  3. Vacuum round. Triggered when the first Enforcer dies or at the start of round 4, whichever comes first. A ship countermeasure can set it off earlier, or a PC can reach the leechcraft's exterior control panel through the breach and tear its clamps free (action, DC 14 STR (Athletics) or INT (Arcana)). For one round only, everyone in the corridor makes a DC 13 STR save or is pulled toward the opening, and a DC 12 DEX save or takes 1d6 bludgeoning from debris. A creature pulled loose is Adrift. Afterward the leechcraft is gone and the pull dies.
  4. End. The enemies are dead and the shuttle bay door opens.
Running it. This fight fields up to 16 devils plus 7 allies and PCs. Roll initiative once per monster type (all Skirmishers act together, and so on). The Skirmishers fly and harass with tail spikes from range. The Wretches swarm into melee and explode in cinders when they die, and the other devils are fire-immune, so they pile in anyway. The Enforcers use their 10-ft. reach and Rally Cry to push the swarm.

Act 2 — Iron Rain onto Dis

A district of Dis and its Arcane Library · ~50–60 min
2.1Planar Jump & Iron Rain
10–15 min · done by ~1:30
Show players: Dis ↗

The ship jumps as the pods launch, which is why the Iron Rain comes in hot and early.

The planar jump is a short rest
  • The jump takes about an hour and counts as a short rest. Hit Dice, short-rest features, Stem Injectors (3 charges), and Pulse Fists (3 charges) all refill.
  • The landing hazard below happens after the rest, on arrival.
  • Pods. Pod 1 carries Servian and the Obsidian PCs. Pod 2 carries Ryn and the Gray PCs. Both land in the same district, but not next to each other.
  • Landing hazard. Each PC makes a DC 13 DEX save as the pod takes fire on the way down. On a failure, the pod lands rough and off-target: 2d6 bludgeoning, and the PC lands separated from their pod-mates. On a success, the landing is clean, with no damage, and the group stays together.
  • Optional. An NPC pod of non-PC Obsidians or Grays fails catastrophically and is lost. It shows real danger without punishing a PC for a bad roll.
  • Dis. Black iron architecture, screaming stonework, a bruised orange sky, and devils being actively broken by the Society's occupation.
  • Solo beats before the pods converge. Ryn seeds suspicion with the Grays. Servian has a command moment with the Obsidians.
  • Stated objective. Retrieve troop-movement intel from the Dis Arcane Library.
2.2The Arcane Library & the Logbook
20–25 min · done by ~1:55
Battle map (player) ↗ Battle map (DM, objectives) ↗ Logbook handout ↗

Layout. The Main Hall is full of bookshelves (three-quarters cover, difficult terrain). The Records Vault behind it holds the archivist's desk, the Logbook, and the Chimera Codex on a warded pedestal.

The Logbook. An Infernal ledger in the Records Vault, right next to the objective. It records every use of the Chimera Codex: the Color, the date, the requesting House, and the soul-cost paid to devil brokers. Low Colors trade their own souls, and both low and high Colors trade the souls of their slaves. One entry names House Ferrox: Servian's cousin, Lucan au Ferrox. Show the handout once the PCs find it.

Three ways to the Logbook

A · Stealth & search
DC 13 DEX (Stealth) group check to reach the vault unseen. On a failure, the guards notice and Scene 2.3 starts. The PCs can still search afterward.
DC 13 INT (Investigation) to find the ledger among the vault's records.
B · The cagey archivist
The archivist denies knowing anything.
DC 13 WIS (Insight) They're lying about the vault.
DC 14 CHA (Deception) to talk your way in, for example by posing as Society auditors. On a failure, the archivist calls the guards and Scene 2.3 starts.
C · A petitioner (no check)
A soul bound to Dis, or a Society slave brought along for labor, has overheard something and points the PCs to the vault, or simply reacts with fear and resignation. It's the human cost made visible.
  • Reading it. Anyone who reads Infernal reads it outright. Otherwise, DC 15 INT (Arcana or Investigation) gets the gist: Colors, Houses, soul-costs. Servian reads Infernal, so if he translates, he finds his cousin's entry himself.
  • Servian's crack. He is genuinely surprised and disgusted, one of the only moments his composure slips. Give it a brief flash, then he re-forms the mask and doubles down on the mission. For more ambiguity, let it visibly shake him going into Act 3.
  • Knowledge. The rest of the party now learns what the Codex really is.

20 min · done by ~2:15

Encounter — Library Guards (lighter than expected)

Before the fight

These are Dis's peacetime library security, not soldiers. They hesitate and ask questions because they don't understand why their Society allies are attacking. A DC 12 WIS (Insight) check reads it: confusion, not hostility. The fight being this easy is a warning sign of its own before Act 3.

Allies — present and fighting

Servian au FerroxAC 20 · HP 130stat block ↗ DM lever: spend his resources here (Lay on Hands, a Pulse Fist charge or two, a smite on a guard) so he walks into Act 3 capable but not at full strength. Ryn GrayAC 16 · HP 85stat block ↗ Still on her Pulse Blade and Pulse Rifle. The Razor stays hidden.

Forces

  • 2 Barbtail Skirmishers + 2 Ash-Bound Wretches. No Enforcer. This is deliberately light and doesn't scale with party size.
  • Optional: the Devil Patrol Leader. A guard captain used to routine duty with an ally, not an invasion. Use the Iron Ward Enforcer stat block, reskinned as Dis city watch. The fight stays light either way.
  • Terrain. The Main Hall bookshelves give three-quarters cover and are difficult terrain.

Optional — Patrol Leader

Securing the Codex

The Chimera Codex sits on a warded pedestal in the Records Vault. Unpicking the ward takes an action and a DC 14 INT (Arcana) check, and the PCs can retry. On a failure, the ward flares and the alarm carries across the district, which works as the reason the horde descends in Act 3.

Act 3 — The Twist & Extraction

The Extraction Plaza, Dis · ~60–70 min
3.1The Reveal & the Order
10–15 min · done by ~2:30
Battle map: Extraction Plaza ↗
  • The horde. A horde of Dis's own devils arrives as the party secures the book. They're reacting to the betrayal in real time: chaotic, alarmed, and hostile to the Society specifically.
  • Ryn turns. She reveals the full truth to everyone (the Logbook, the pipeline, what it costs the people at the bottom) and sides against the Society. She draws a Razor of her own. She isn't asking the party to follow her, only to choose.
  • Servian's order. With total calm, he orders the party to kill Ryn and the devils. To him it isn't murder, it's maintenance of the system. He draws his razor.
  • The stakes. Both leaders are equally lethal, so the choice is about loyalty, not safety.
Oathbound Compulsion
  • Every Obsidian and Gray PC bears two sigils, one on each hand, binding them to Servian's direct spoken orders.
  • Trigger. A PC takes an action that directly defies his order. The compulsion is passive and takes no action to resist. It fires the moment defiance happens.
  • 1st break (right hand). 2d6 psychic damage (DC 14 CON save for half) and disadvantage on the next roll.
  • 2nd break (left hand). 2d6 psychic damage (DC 14 CON save for half), disadvantage on the next roll, and 1 level of exhaustion. After the first break, the PC's next defiant action breaks the second sigil automatically. No new order is needed.
  • Both broken. The PC is free of the compulsion for the rest of the encounter.
  • Narrate each sigil visibly cracking or burning out.

Encounter — The Final Horde

The plaza

An iron courtyard. Iron pillars give half cover. A narrow chokepoint leads to the extraction shuttle. It's the bottleneck, and it's the only way out for anyone running with the book.

The leaders

Servian au FerroxAC 20 · HP 130stat block ↗ Razor drawn, the "boss reveal" of the fight. Remember any resources he spent in Act 2. Ryn GrayAC 16 · HP 85stat block ↗ Now on her Razor. Drawing it should land as a visible escalation. She still has 3 Pulse Grenades.

Who fights whom

Society path · party sides with Servian
Servian fights alongside the party against Ryn and the full horde. The devils follow Ryn.
Rising path · party turns on Servian
The devils attack Servian and anyone in Society armor, but never Ryn. In round 1, one Skirmisher turns on Servian and fights with the party. The fight ends when Servian falls or is driven off.
Run with the book
The PCs break both sigils, abandon both sides, and make for the shuttle through the chokepoint.

Tested at 4–5 PCs: both paths land at about a 55% chance that at least one PC drops, and deaths are rare.

3.3Endings & Wrap-Up
10 min · done by ~3:25

The one-shot ends at the close of the climax combat.

  • Society wins. Servian keeps the book, and Ryn and the Rising are defeated.
  • Rising wins. Ryn's side secures the book, and Servian is defeated or driven off.
  • The PCs escape with the book. The party breaks the oath, abandons both sides, and flees Dis with the Chimera Codex in hand.
