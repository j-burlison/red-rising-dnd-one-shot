BENEDICTION OF DIS

Command center for running the one-shot. Storyboard, mechanics, NPCs, monsters, pacing, and every linked handout in one place.

Storyboard

Full detail: storyboard.html

Act 1 — The Benediction (aboard The Concordia)

  • Cold open: Benediction ceremony, Obsidians kneeling, Grays at the margins, "too quiet" unease. Short RP/skill scene.
  • Break: leechcraft breach through portals. "All units to launch bays. Prepare for planar jump and Iron Rain."
  • Both squads reach the corridor together — ship shakes, hull visibly cut open, gravity fails, roll initiative.
  • Servian and Ryn fight alongside the party in the corridor. Servian's gravity boots let him ignore zero-G until they fail.
  • Round 1: leechcraft carves through the hull; Barbtail Skirmishers and the first Ash-Bound Wretches pour in. Round 2: the Iron Ward Enforcers follow with the rest of the swarm.
  • Mid-fight: leechcraft tears free (Enforcer's death / countermeasure / player action / or at the start of round 4) — one sharp round of vacuum pull + debris.
  • Zero-G Corridor rules apply throughout (see Mechanics).
  • Ends when enemies are dead and the shuttle bay door opens. The planar jump that follows is a short rest — HD, short-rest features, Stem Injectors, and Pulse Fists all refill before the landing hazard on arrival.
  • 1–2 PCs (Gold's confidants) know the true objective now; everyone else believes it's standard intel extraction.

Act 2 — Iron Rain onto Dis

  • Scattered landing across a district of Dis — black iron architecture, screaming stonework, bruised orange sky.
  • Stated objective: retrieve troop movement intel from the Dis Arcane Library.
  • Discovery beat: remaining PCs learn the Chimera Codex's true nature via the Logbook — found near the objective, via Investigation/stealth or a social check against a cagey archivist/guard.
  • Combat: library guards, deliberately lighter than expected (2 Barbtail Skirmishers + 2 Ash-Bound Wretches, no Enforcer) — sells the "unprepared ally" reframe.
  • DM lever: have Servian spend resources here (Lay on Hands, a Pulse Fist charge, a smite) to weaken him ahead of Act 3.

Act 3 — The Twist and Extraction

  • A devil horde arrives as the party secures the Chimera Codex.
  • Ryn Gray turns, reveals the truth to everyone, sides against the Society.
  • Servian orders the party to kill Ryn and the devils — every PC is Oathbound and must actively break the oath to defy this.
  • Both leaders draw razors — Servian and Ryn are equally lethal, so the choice is about loyalty, not safety.
  • Climax combat: PCs choose a side, or break the oath entirely and run with the book. If the party turns on Servian, one Barbtail Skirmisher turns on him too in round 1.
  • Ends at the close of combat.
Three endings: Society wins (Servian keeps the book) · Rising wins (Ryn's side secures it) · PCs escape with the book (break the oath, abandon both sides).

Mechanics Quick Reference

Full detail: lore-mechanics.html
Oathbound Compulsion

Two sigils, one per hand. Each break: 2d6 psychic damage (Con save DC 14 for half) + disadvantage on next roll. 2nd break also costs 1 exhaustion. The next defiant action automatically breaks the 2nd sigil — no new order required. After both sigils break, that PC is free of the compulsion for the rest of the encounter.

Zero-G Corridor (Act 1)

25 ft. wide, 120 ft. long, 15 ft. ceiling, gentle bend. Movement: push off (Str/Athletics DC 12) to drift in a straight line to next anchor. Anchors every 10 ft. on both walls + ceiling. Vacuum pull round (triggers when the first Enforcer dies, or at the start of round 4, whichever comes first — a countermeasure or PC reaching the control panel can also trigger it earlier): Str save DC 13 or pulled toward breach; debris: Dex save DC 12 or 1d6 bludgeoning. Recoil: unbraced attacks push the attacker 5 ft. away from their target. A creature left unbraced after a failed push-off, recoil, or the vacuum pull is Adrift until the start of its next turn — melee attacks against an Adrift creature have advantage. Devils move freely, never Adrift, unaffected by these rules.

Stem Injectors (universal)

3 charges/short rest. Bonus action. Each charge = Greater Healing Potion (4d4+4). An ally within 5 ft. can trigger a downed or incapacitated creature's injector as an action (one charge from the wearer's armor).

Gravity Boots (Gold only)

Functions as Boots of Flying — flying speed = normal speed, ignores zero-G rules. 2 charges, recharge on long rest. Servian wears these into Act 1 — consider having them run out or take damage mid-fight so he's grounded like everyone else for the rest of that encounter.

War Chant (Obsidian)

Bonus action to start; Concentration. Self + allies within 15 ft. immune to frightened. At the start of the Obsidian's turns, weak creatures (CR 1 or lower — Ash-Bound Wretches) within 30 ft. that can hear it: Wisdom save (DC 8 + prof + Con) or frightened until the Obsidian's next turn.

Razor (Gold only)

1d8 slashing, finesse, light. Treats the target as unarmored: its AC is what it would be without armor (10 + its Dexterity modifier, or its Unarmored Defense if it has that feature), plus any shield and magic bonuses. Worn armor and natural armor don't count. Whip Mode: item interaction to toggle (free) — reach 10 ft., 1d6 slashing, loses light; hits can disarm/trip at range instead of dealing damage. Both Servian and Ryn carry one — Ryn's stays hidden (Pulse Blade instead) until her Act 3 reveal.

NPCs

Full stat blocks: npc-and-monsters.html
Servian au Ferrox Gold Paladin 7 · CR 6 · AC 20 · HP 130

Cassius-style cold composure — precise, unshaken, never raises his voice. Believes the Chimera Codex was meant to be destroyed, not exploited. Genuinely disgusted when his cousin's House (Lucan au Ferrox) surfaces in the Logbook — one of the only moments his mask slips.

Multiattack: 2 razor attacks (+9, 1d8+5, treats target as unarmored)
Divine Smite: +2d8 radiant/slot on razor hit
Spell Slots: 4 × 1st, 3 × 2nd
Pulse Fist: 3 charges/short rest, bonus action, 15-ft. cone, 2d6 force + push (DC 15 Dex)
Bred to Command: 3/short rest, bonus action, ally advantage
Aura of Protection: +4 saves within 10 ft.
Lay on Hands: 35 HP pool
Peerless Reflexes: 1/day, turn a failed save into a success
Gravity Boots: Boots of Flying, 2 charges
DM lever: spend his resources on Act 2 guards so he's weaker going into Act 3.
Ryn Gray Gray Ranger 7 · CR 6 · AC 16 · HP 85

Holiday-style bluntness and loyalty — plainspoken, protective of her people over rank. Has been quietly recruiting/vetting the party since Act 1. The betrayal is personal, not calculated.

Multiattack: 2 attacks — Pulse Blade (+8, 1d8+4) until reveal, then Razor (+8, 1d8+4, treats target as unarmored); or Pulse Rifle (+8, 1d8+4, 150/600)
Pulse Grenade: x3, thrown 60 ft., 15-ft. radius, 3d8 force (DC 15 Dex)
Hunter's Mark: 1/short rest, no slot, +1d6 on marked target
Pack Tactics / Tracker's Eye / +2 Initiative
Keep the Razor hidden until the Act 3 reveal — drawing it should land as a visible escalation at the table.
