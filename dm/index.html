BENEDICTION OF DIS

Run the session top to bottom from this page. Every scene has its checks, encounter numbers, devil stat blocks, and time budget inline. Servian's and Ryn's stat blocks open in a new tab.

Overview

Premise · who knows what · session clock

Premise. Obsidian and Gray operators under Servian au Ferrox, a Peerless Scarred Gold, drop onto Dis, capital of Dispater. Their stated orders are to extract intel on troop movements. The real target is the Chimera Codex, a spellbook that lets Colors bargain with devils for cross-color breeding, or to change their own Color outright, getting around the Society's caste laws. Dis has long been a Society ally, so the Iron Rain is a betrayal, not a follow-up to a war.

Who knows. Servian knows the true objective from the start and shares it with no one. Ryn Gray, the Gray Troupe Leader, knows it through Rising channels and is a Rising agent. Two players are set up before the session. "Irina Gray" is a Rising agent and knows the true objective; her written Rising orders put the Logbook first and the Codex second. Smack "Eldorf" Gray has a hint: Ryn asked him to have her back, and told him that if she says anything about breaking something, that's his cue. Maartog and everyone else believe it's intel extraction until the Logbook in Act 2.

Act 1 — The Benediction

Aboard The Concordia, in orbit above Farun · 110 min
1.1The Benediction Ceremony
50 min · done by ~1:05
Show players: The Concordia ↗ Show players: Servian ↗ Show players: Ryn ↗
  1. The Grays' overlook room (~25 min). The session opens with the Gray squad in a room overlooking the ceremony hall. Ryn briefs the Grays here with the cover story: troop-movement intel from one of the Arcane Libraries of Dis. Holiday-style bluntness. She talks to Grays and Obsidians as people, not ranks, and the contrast with Servian is worth playing up. She's quietly probing 1–2 PCs' loyalties while she does it. Banter follows, and the Gray players introduce their characters in character: name, Color, class, and the hook they brought (step 6 of the player briefing).
  2. Cut to the floor (~25 min). The Obsidians kneel on one knee beside a standing Servian while the Whites perform the Benediction. There's unease in the air because Dis has been "too quiet." The Obsidian players introduce their characters here, as narration or inner voice, since Obsidians can't speak out of turn.
    • Caste roleplay. Obsidians can't speak out of turn. Grays needle at the edges.
    • The Obsidians get no brief. They kneel, take the blessing, and will be told what to do when it's time. Orders, not explanations.
    • Servian gives no brief. Cold, Cassius-style composure. Clipped, formal command language, and he never raises his voice. He knows the true objective and shares it with no one.
  3. The turned backs. When the Whites name "Maartog, son of Slate," the kneeling Obsidian NPCs turn their backs on him in a ritual shunning. Maartog kneels, but he never bows his head to a Gold.
  4. Servian's brothers. As the ceremony ends, Servian's brothers approach him: Karnas au Ferrox, absolutely massive and a brute, and Cagney au Ferrox, skinny, lithe and a trickster. They impress on him how much the mission matters. It has to be him, because no one will suspect him. The Sovereign herself is depending on him, and so is the honor of their family. Both of them know about their father Lucan's soul trades; Servian doesn't. (Optional: Maartog, kneeling nearby, overhears. It's a hint, not the objective.)
  5. Show the oath working. Give a small order that an Obsidian visibly can't disobey, or let a PC flinch when tempted to. Players should know the sigils are watching before Act 3. Full rules are in Scene 3.1.
  6. Pre-game knowledge. Irina (the Rising agent) and Smack (the hint) were set up before the session. Give them room to act on what they know without forcing it. Her sigils are disabled, cut by her pact or by the Rising, so the compulsion has no hold on her. Whenever she gets a direct order, she either pretends she's still bound or defies it openly and breaks her cover.

Skill scene: who's suspicious of the mission?

  • DC 12 WIS (Insight) In the overlook room: the brief is thin for a strike on an ally's capital. Ryn is reciting the official line.
  • DC 15 WIS (Insight) In the overlook room: Ryn is sizing up specific operatives while she briefs, not just running through the plan. This seeds Act 3.
  • DC 14 CHA (Persuasion or Deception) against Servian, for anyone who presses him on the floor or after the ceremony (an Obsidian doing so is still speaking out of turn). He won't name the objective, but he confirms there's more to this mission than intel and that it's above their clearance.
  • Ryn talks to anyone who speaks to her plainly, with no check needed. She asks more questions than she answers.

Break. Hellish leechcraft breach through portals in space and devils board The Concordia. Shipwide: "All units to launch bays. Prepare for planar jump and Iron Rain."

1.2Breach in the Zero-G Corridor
60 min · done by ~2:05
Battle map: Zero-G Corridor ↗ Show players: devil art ↗

Both squads reach the corridor between the cargo hold and the launch bay together. The ship shakes, everyone watches the hull get cut open from outside, and gravity fails. Roll initiative.

Encounter — Ship-Boarding Party

The corridor

25 ft. wide, 120 ft. long, 15 ft. ceiling, with a gentle bend. Handholds (anchors) every 10 ft. on both walls and the ceiling. The breach opens on the south wall in round 1. The cargo hold is at one end and the launch bay at the other. The map has no hidden info, so it's safe to screen-share.

Allies — fighting alongside the party

Servian au FerroxAC 20 · HP 130stat block ↗ His gravity boots (2 charges) let him ignore zero-G. Have them run out or get damaged mid-fight by a debris strike, a solid hit, or the breach event, so he's grounded like everyone else. The damaged boots can carry into Act 3 as flavor. Ryn GrayAC 16 · HP 85stat block ↗ Fights with her Pulse Blade and Pulse Rifle. Her Razor stays hidden until Act 3.

Rule of thumb: Skirmishers = PCs + 2. Enforcers = 3 (2 at three PCs). Wretches = 6.

Zero-G rules (whole fight)
  • Movement. No walking. Push off a surface with a DC 12 STR (Athletics) check to drift your speed in a straight line until you hit something or reach an anchor. Changing direction needs a new anchor.
  • Anchors. The handholds every 10 ft. define the viable route.
  • Recoil. Any weapon attack, melee or ranged, made while not braced against an anchor pushes the attacker 5 ft. directly away from the target after it resolves. Bracing cancels it.
  • Adrift. A creature left unbraced after a failed push-off, a recoil, or the vacuum pull is Adrift until the start of its next turn. Melee attacks against an Adrift creature have advantage.
  • Devils move and reposition freely and are never Adrift.
  • Caste flavor. Obsidians push off harder and resist the pull (Strength). Grays thread debris and stick anchor grabs (Dexterity).

Round by round

  1. Round 1. The leechcraft carves fully through the hull. The Skirmishers and the first Wretches pour through the breach.
  2. Round 2. The Enforcers follow with the rest of the Wretches.
  3. Vacuum round. Triggered when the first Enforcer dies or at the start of round 4, whichever comes first. A ship countermeasure can set it off earlier, or a PC can reach the leechcraft's exterior control panel through the breach and tear its clamps free (action, DC 14 STR (Athletics) or INT (Arcana)). For one round only, everyone in the corridor makes a DC 13 STR save or is pulled toward the opening, and a DC 12 DEX save or takes 1d6 bludgeoning from debris. A creature pulled loose is Adrift. Afterward the leechcraft is gone and the pull dies.
  4. End. The enemies are dead and the shuttle bay door opens.
Running it. This fight fields up to 16 devils plus 7 allies and PCs. Roll initiative once per monster type (all Skirmishers act together, and so on). The Skirmishers fly and harass with tail spikes from range. The Wretches swarm into melee and explode in cinders when they die, and the other devils are fire-immune, so they pile in anyway. The Enforcers use their 10-ft. reach and Rally Cry to push the swarm.

Act 2 — Iron Rain onto Dis

A district of Dis and one of its Arcane Libraries · 80 min
2.1Planar Jump & Iron Rain
20 min · done by ~2:25
Show players: Dis ↗

The Concordia jumps as the drop ships launch, which is why the Iron Rain comes in hot and early.

The planar jump is a short rest
  • The jump only takes a few minutes, but time and space warp as the Concordia makes it, so the party gets the benefits of a short rest. Hit Dice, short-rest features, Stem Injectors (3 charges), and Pulse Fists (3 charges) all refill.
  • The crash landing below happens after the rest, on arrival.
  1. One drop ship. Both squads ride down together, Servian, Ryn, and every PC, in a single drop ship flown by a Blue pilot.
  2. Launch. As they clear the bay, the drop ship beside theirs is hit by a massive bolt of infernal magic from the city below and explodes.
  3. The starShell. Through the viewports they watch a Gold in a starShell, heavy mechanized armor, get tackled midair by a massive winged devil. The two tumble out of sight together.
  4. The warlock beat. As the drop ship falls into Dis, every warlock's patron speaks up in their head, angry but amused that the Society has come to strike Dis: "The hubris, to come here and turn upon your ally." This covers Irina and any other warlock at the table.
  5. The spear. Partway down, another massive winged devil hurls a magically charged spear through the cockpit window and kills the Blue. The drop ship goes down.
  6. Crash landing. Each PC makes a DC 13 DEX save as the drop ship hits. On a failure, they take 2d6 bludgeoning and are thrown clear of the wreck, separated from the squad. On a success, they brace in time, take no damage, and climb out with the group. Servian and Ryn both walk away from the crash.
  • Dis. Black iron architecture, screaming stonework, a bruised orange sky, and devils being actively broken by the Society's occupation.
  • In the wreckage. Ryn checks on the Grays and quietly seeds suspicion. Servian has a command moment with the Obsidians and gives them their first orders: secure the library.
  • Stated objective. Retrieve troop-movement intel from one of the Arcane Libraries of Dis.
2.2The Arcane Library & the Logbook
35 min · done by ~3:00
Battle map (player) ↗ Battle map (DM, objectives) ↗ Logbook handout ↗ Show players: Scrivax ↗

Exterior. One of several Arcane Libraries in Dis: a narrow tower of black iron plates riveted together like the pages of a closed book, its upper windows glowing a dull furnace-orange.

The front door is guarded by two Barbtail Skirmishers. They're the two Skirmishers from Scene 2.3: going in the front by force starts that fight at the door, and if the fight breaks out inside instead, they come through the front door at the start of round 2.

The petitioners' door. Around the back, a narrow, unmarked iron door lets bound souls file their contracts straight into the Records Vault, skipping the Main Hall. It isn't on the battle map; place it on the vault's back wall. PCs circling the building spot it with a DC 13 WIS (Perception) or INT (Investigation), or a petitioner (path C) can point it out. It's unguarded but locked: DC 13 DEX (thieves' tools) opens it quietly.

Layout. The Main Hall is full of bookshelves (three-quarters cover, difficult terrain). The Records Vault behind it holds the archivist's desk, the Logbook, and the Chimera Codex on a warded pedestal.

The Logbook. An Infernal ledger in the Records Vault, right next to the objective. It records every use of the Chimera Codex: the Color, the date, the requesting House, and the soul-cost paid to devil brokers. Most entries are cross-Color unions; some are Color changes. Low Colors trade their own souls, and both low and high Colors trade the souls of their slaves. Two entries name House Ferrox: Servian's father, Lucan au Ferrox. His cross-Color unions produced children: Servian's own half-siblings. Tucked in the back is a loose older page, about 20 years old. It records House Ferrox selling Slate's tribe, 40 Obsidian souls, and branding the chief an oathbreaker. It proves Maartog's father was framed. (Optional: the Ferrox household slaves who raised Maartog are among Lucan's "6 slave souls (mixed stock)" from a year ago.) Show the handout once the PCs find it; the loose page is on it.

The approach is the players' choice. They can sneak in, talk their way in, or go in by force; force just starts Scene 2.3 early. The devils know a Society attack is coming, but not that this group is after the Codex and the Logbook. Smack's infiltration skills are one option if the table wants a quiet route; nothing depends on them.

Three ways to the Logbook

A · Stealth & search
DC 13 DEX (Stealth) group check to reach the vault unseen, through the petitioners' door or past the guards out front. On a failure, the guards notice and Scene 2.3 starts. The PCs can still search afterward.
DC 13 INT (Investigation) to find the ledger among the vault's records.
B · The cagey archivist
The archivist, Scrivax Vellumhorn, denies knowing anything. A spindly, horned devil-scholar in spectacles, forever juggling a tower of books.
DC 13 WIS (Insight) They're lying about the vault.
DC 14 CHA (Deception) to talk your way in, for example by posing as Society auditors. On a failure, the archivist calls the guards and Scene 2.3 starts.
He is Irina's devil broker. He brokered her deal: for a fragment of her soul, the Codex changed her from Copper to Gray, and the same bargain gave her warlock power. He can see that fragment on her. In front of the squad he calls her "Administrator" unless she shuts him down with a DC 14 CHA (Deception). Privately he tells her the term is now due, and offers to forgive the remainder if the Codex stays in Dis.
C · A petitioner (no check)
A soul bound to Dis, or a Society slave brought along for labor, has overheard something and points the PCs to the vault, or simply reacts with fear and resignation. It's the human cost made visible.
  • Reading it. Anyone who reads Infernal reads it outright. Otherwise, DC 15 INT (Arcana or Investigation) gets the gist: Colors, Houses, soul-costs. Servian reads Infernal, so if he translates, he finds his father's entries himself.
  • Servian's crack. He is genuinely surprised and disgusted, one of the only moments his composure slips. He finds the Slate page at the same moment as his father's entries, so he learns his House has been doing this for a generation. Give it a brief flash, then he re-forms the mask and doubles down on the mission. For more ambiguity, let it visibly shake him going into Act 3.
  • Irina's own entry. One row reads "Administrator, Copper: 1 fragment, own soul. Color change, Copper to Gray. Remainder due at term." That's Irina. The row doesn't name her, but her secret is in the book she's about to carry.
  • Irina carries the Logbook out of the vault. She may be the one who finds it. Otherwise Ryn or Servian can order her to carry it; that's your call at the table.
  • Knowledge. The rest of the party now learns what the Codex really is.

Before the fight

These are Dis's peacetime library security, not soldiers. They hesitate and ask questions because they don't understand why their Society allies are attacking. A DC 12 WIS (Insight) check reads it: confusion, not hostility. The fight being this easy is a warning sign of its own before Act 3.

Forces

  • Terrain. The Main Hall bookshelves give three-quarters cover and are difficult terrain.

Securing the Codex

The Chimera Codex sits on a warded pedestal in the Records Vault. Unpicking the ward takes an action and a DC 14 INT (Arcana) check, and the PCs can retry. On a failure, the ward flares and the alarm carries across the district, which works as the reason the horde descends in Act 3. Once the ward is down, Ryn takes the Codex; she needs it in hand for Scene 3.1.

Act 3 — The Twist & Extraction

The Extraction Plaza, Dis · 95 min
3.1The Reveal & the Order
20 min · done by ~3:45
Battle map: Extraction Plaza ↗
  1. The Logbook demand. On the way to the shuttle, at the Extraction Plaza, Servian stops: "Give me the Logbook. I'm going to burn it." It incriminates his family.
  2. Irina's bind. Irina is carrying it. Her sigils are disabled, so the order has no hold on her, but nobody else knows that. She can hand it over and play bound, fake a sigil breaking and refuse, or defy him openly with no pain at all, which blows her cover before anyone else moves.
  3. Maartog's stake. The proof of his father's innocence is in that book. Servian can offer to spare that one page, and to restore Slate's name before the Sovereign, if Maartog stands with him.
  4. "Break the chains." Ryn answers with the call sign. Irina recognizes it at once. Smack recognizes his cue ("breaking something") but not what it means, until he realizes his closest friend has lied to him for ten years and is calling in his promise.
  5. Ryn turns. She reveals the full truth to everyone (the Logbook, the pipeline, what it costs the people at the bottom) and sides against the Society. She draws a Razor of her own. She isn't asking the party to follow her, only to choose. She throws Smack the Codex: "Get this to the Rising, whatever happens to me." That puts the escape ending in a player's hands.
  6. The horde. Dis's devils, drawn by the alarm and the attack, pour into the plaza at Ryn's reveal. They're chaotic, alarmed, and hostile to the Society specifically, never Ryn.
  7. Servian's order. With total calm, he orders the party to kill Ryn and the devils. To him it isn't murder, it's maintenance of the system. He draws his razor.
  8. Choices at the table.
    • Irina chooses whether to unmask as Rising or stay covered, playing bound to Servian's orders, and walk out with the Logbook.
    • Maartog. Ryn tells him, "Your father's people were sold with that book. Your gods sold them." He also has a Worf-style option: let the page burn and serve anyway. If he breaks his oath, he wraps the banner scrap over the burning sigil.
  9. The stakes. Both leaders are equally lethal, so the choice is about loyalty, not safety.
Oathbound Compulsion
  • Every Obsidian and Gray PC bears two sigils, one on each hand, binding them to Servian's direct spoken orders.
  • Except Irina. Her sigils are disabled (by her pact or by the Rising). Orders have no hold on her, so she takes no damage, but defying one openly breaks her cover. To stay hidden she has to act bound, or fake a sigil breaking.
  • Trigger. A PC takes an action that directly defies his order. The compulsion is passive and takes no action to resist. It fires the moment defiance happens.
  • 1st break (right hand). 2d6 psychic damage (DC 14 CON save for half) and disadvantage on the next roll.
  • 2nd break (left hand). 2d6 psychic damage (DC 14 CON save for half), disadvantage on the next roll, and 1 level of exhaustion. After the first break, the PC's next defiant action breaks the second sigil automatically. No new order is needed.
  • Both broken. The PC is free of the compulsion for the rest of the encounter.
  • Narrate each sigil visibly cracking or burning out.

3.3Endings & Wrap-Up
15 min · done by ~5:00

The one-shot ends at the close of the climax combat.

  • Society wins. Servian keeps the book, and Ryn and the Rising are defeated.
  • Rising wins. Ryn's side secures the book, and Servian is defeated or driven off.
  • The PCs escape with the book. The party breaks the oath, abandons both sides, and flees Dis with the Chimera Codex in hand.
