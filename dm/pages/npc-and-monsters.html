NPCs & MONSTERS

Naming convention: Golds carry House names ("au [House]" — e.g., Servian au Ferrox). All other Colors take their Color as a surname (e.g., Ryn Gray).

NPCs

Servian au Ferrox, Peerless Scarred
Class: Paladin  |  Combat role: heavy melee, razor-wielder in Act 3, Bred to Command support

A Peerless Scarred commander, bred and trained since birth for exactly this kind of operation — decisive, controlled, and utterly convinced of the righteousness of the Society's order. Doesn't relish cruelty, which makes him scarier: every order he gives in Act 3, including "kill the Gray," is delivered with total calm, because to him it isn't murder, it's maintenance of the system that keeps the galaxy — and now the planes — from chaos. (Play him with a Cassius au Bellona-style cold composure: precise, unshaken, dangerous specifically because he never seems to struggle.)

  • Personal stake: knows the true objective from the start — the mission is to retrieve the Chimera Codex and bring the cross-color breeding pipeline fully "in house." He believes the book was meant to be destroyed, not exploited; he does not know the true scale of its use.
  • The cousin twist: when the Logbook surfaces his cousin's House, Servian is genuinely surprised and disgusted — a real crack in his composure, one of the only moments his control visibly slips.
  • Roleplay hook: clipped, formal command language even under pressure; never raises his voice
  • In Act 3: draws a razor once the party's loyalty is in question — the "boss reveal" of the fight
Ryn Gray, Rising Agent
Class: Ranger  |  Combat role: skirmisher, razor-wielder in Act 3, leads the Rising/devil defense

Been running this troupe long enough that the Grays trust her instincts without question — which is exactly what let her quietly recruit and vet the party over the course of the mission without anyone noticing. Genuinely cares about the Grays under her command; the betrayal in Act 3 isn't cold calculation like the Gold's orders, it's personal. (Play her with a Holiday-style bluntness and loyalty — plainspoken, protective of her people over any chain of command, quietly formidable rather than showy about it.)

  • Personal stake: knows the true objective via Rising channels from the start; has likely been probing 1-2 PCs' loyalties since Act 1's ceremony scene
  • Roleplay hook: talks to Grays and Obsidians alike as people rather than ranks — the contrast between her command style and Servian's is worth playing up throughout
  • In Act 3: reveals the truth when the devil horde arrives, draws a razor of her own, makes it clear she's not asking the party to follow her — just choose
Devil Patrol Leader
Act 2, Library Guards  |  Suggested type: Bearded devil or similar mid-tier fiend, reskinned as Dis city watch

Not expecting a fight — this is a guard captain used to ally-status routine, not an invasion footing, which is exactly why the Act 2 encounter should read as "lighter than expected." Could have a brief, almost confused moment before combat (Insight-worthy) — they genuinely don't understand why Society forces are attacking.

Key Petitioners / Slaves
Logbook context, Act 2 flavor

Non-combat NPCs encountered near the library or archive — souls or servants (mortal petitioners bound to Dis, or Society slaves brought along for labor) who can be a source of the moral wrinkle. Consider one who's overheard something about the Logbook and can point PCs toward it, or who simply reacts with fear/resignation when PCs appear.

  • 4x Ash-Bound Wretch
  • 2x Barbtail Skirmisher

  • 3x Ash-Bound Wretch
  • 3x Barbtail Skirmisher
  • 2x Iron Ward Enforcer
